## 💡 Pro Tips for L4 Success

1. **Pattern Recognition is Key:** Don't memorize solutions, learn to identify patterns
2. **Trade-offs Matter:** In system design, there's no "perfect" answer - articulate trade-offs
3. **Code Quality Counts:** Write production-ready code with proper variable names, edge cases
4. **Think Out Loud:** Interviewers want to see your thought process
5. **Ask Clarifying Questions:** Shows senior-level thinking
6. **Scale Matters:** Always consider how your solution scales to millions/billions
7. **Own Your Stories:** Be authentic in behavioral rounds, own failures
8. **Practice Whiteboarding:** Get comfortable designing on whiteboards/virtual boards
